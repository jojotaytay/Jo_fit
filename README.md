# Jo Fit ✦ Calisthenics Quest

A tiny progressive workout app built around two realistic weekly sessions, calisthenics skill quests, flexibility, and celebrating what your body learns to do.

## Use
Open the GitHub Pages site, choose Tuesday or Thursday, and follow the workout. Progress is stored locally in your browser.

### Main quests
- Tuesday: push + legs + L-sit
- Thursday: pull + legs + hanging
- Saturday: optional run/walk
- 15-minute rescue workout for chaotic days

The app intentionally keeps weight tracking secondary to strength and skill progress. Exercise targets should be pain-free; stop and seek appropriate professional advice for pain, dizziness, faintness, or other concerning symptoms.
